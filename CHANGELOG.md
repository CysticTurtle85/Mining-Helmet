# Changelog

## 1.1.0
- Now available for Fabric, Quilt, NeoForge and Forge, on Minecraft 1.20.1, 1.21.1, 1.21.4, 1.21.5, 1.21.8, 1.21.10, 1.21.11, 26.1.2, 26.2 and 26.3.
- Light works with current LambDynamicLights releases (updated to its new light-source format).
- Forge 1.20.1 and NeoForge 1.21.4 use Tschipcraft's Dynamic Lights, which works in singleplayer and on servers.
- Sodium Dynamic Lights also works on Forge 1.20.1 (optional) and now lights up straight away, without needing F3+T.
- Dyeing uses the vanilla dye system, so the helmet can be dyed like leather armour.
